## About Me

I am a second-year student studying **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning** and **Computer Engineering** at Bursa Technical University.

I am passionate about artificial intelligence, machine learning, and software development. My goal is to strengthen my theoretical knowledge through hands-on experience, research, and real-world projects.

I have worked on training and developing machine learning models, including **supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and YOLO-based object detection and tracking systems**.

I am also a member of the software team of the **Poyraz Team**, operating under the Aerospace and Defense Industry Society at our university.

Within the team, I focus on computer vision, deep learning model training, and object detection and tracking for UAV applications. I also work on deploying and optimizing models for embedded systems such as the Raspberry Pi 5.

Through academic studies, personal projects, and teamwork, I continuously develop my technical skills while improving my problem-solving, collaboration, and engineering abilities.
